# Pizza dough
A simple dough for two pizzas. Start __one day__ before you bake.
## Ingredients
* 500 g flour, type _00_
+ 325 g water
* 10 g salt
## Steps
1. Mix the flour and the water.
2. Rest the dough for 24 hours.
#### Baking
